# Gameplay Documentation & Level Breakdown - Parallel Worlds

## 1. Core Mechanics
In **Parallel Worlds**, the player's primary goal is to heal the damaged Reality World and observe how every positive eco-action transforms the parallel Fantasy Realm from 0% to 100% Growth.

## 2. Complete 10-Level Breakdown

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Barren Wasteland | Tree Planting | Scorched deforested dirt | Towering bioluminescent World Tree |
