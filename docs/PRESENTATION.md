# IIT National Gamethon Presentation Guide - Parallel Worlds

## 1. Pitch Slide Structure
- **Slide 1: Title & Hook**
  - "Parallel Worlds: What if every tree you plant in reality creates a magical sky tree in a parallel realm?"
- **Slide 2: Problem Statement**
  - Environmental apathy among youth due to lack of immediate, tangible cause-and-effect feedback.
- **Slide 3: Solution & Gameplay Concept**
  - A dual-world browser game connecting real eco-restoration actions with bioluminescent fantasy transformations across 10 levels.
- **Slide 4: Technical Innovation**
  - Web Audio API procedural sound synthesizer.
  - Interactive HTML5 Canvas particle engine (fireflies, blooming pollen).
  - 10 distinct physics/puzzle level mechanics.
- **Slide 5: Live Showcase & Demo Steps**
  1. Show dual-world slider on Landing Page.
  2. Select Guardian character.
  3. Play Level 1 (Tree Planting) & Level 4 (Stream Pipe Puzzle).
  4. Demonstrate Portal Shift (Reality -> Fantasy Realm at 100% Growth).
  5. Showcase IIT Gamethon Leaderboard & Certificate of Completion.
